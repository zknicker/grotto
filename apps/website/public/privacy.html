Privacy

Last updated July 29, 2026.

Grotto data

Grotto stores shared conversations, memberships, tasks, reminders, connection settings, and connected-service credentials on the hosted Grotto Server. Agent workspaces, execution credentials, and model-provider credentials stay on the attached Computer that runs the Agent.

Connected services

When you connect an MCP server, its authorization service may provide Grotto with access tokens and account information. Grotto Server stores the credentials needed to make authorized requests and does not expose raw credentials to Agents.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@grotto.sh.
